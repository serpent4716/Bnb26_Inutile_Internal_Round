Most people think the secret to getting rich is cutting out your morning coffee. It is not. That advice is mathematically useless.
Here is why. If you skip a ₹200 coffee every day, you save about ₹70,000 a year. That sounds like a lot, until you compare it to one bad investment.
Last year I lost ₹50,000 on a stock tip from a friend. One tip. Gone in three weeks.
Here is the one rule I follow now. Never invest in something you cannot explain in one sentence. If you cannot explain how it makes money, you are not investing. You are gambling.
The second thing that actually moved the needle for me was automating my savings. The day my salary lands, 20% goes straight into an index fund. I never see it, so I never miss it. In two years that one change saved me more than every coffee I have ever bought.
Stop optimizing the small stuff and fix the big decisions. Your rent, your investments, your income. That is where the real money is.
